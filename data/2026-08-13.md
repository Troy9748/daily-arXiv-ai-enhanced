Abstract: It is sometimes suggested that the most massive quasars at high redshift may have formed from rare high-sigma peaks in the cosmic density field. We explore here the evolution in a massive primordial halo corresponding to one of these rare sigma peaks, employing cosmological high-resolution magneto-hydrodynamical zoom-in simulations with initial field strength of $10^{-14}-10^{-8}$ G. The dark matter halo forms at the intersection of strongly convergent flows, leading to the formation of highly supersonic turbulence already on intergalactic scales, with turbulent Mach numbers of order $10-20$ also within the halo. Turbulent magnetic field amplification has never been explored in this regime and we therefore investigate whether this gives rise to effects similar to those observed in more typical halos. While the weaker initial field is somewhat more strongly amplified as a result of the shear flows, overall the evolution in the different simulations is rather similar and the flows are dominated by supersonic turbulence. We show in particular that the turbulent Jeans mass always dominates over the thermal and magnetic Jeans masses, and only on scales of $10^{-2}-10$ pc, the magnetic Jeans mass may become comparable to the thermal one. Our simulations thus strongly suggest the evolution to be dominated by the large-scale dynamics. As established in previous work, we thus expect the formation of central massive objects of a few times $10^4$ M$_\odot$ also in the presence of magnetic fields. The situation is somewhat different from more typical atomic cooling halos, where previous results have indicated a larger relevance of the magnetic Jeans mass on intermediate scales, potentially enhancing the mass of the massive object.

### [2] [Testing Covarying Coupling Constants (CCC) against the full SPARC rotation-curve sample: a like-for-like comparison with MOND and NFW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11575)
*Rajendra P. Gupta,Nikolaos Samaras*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

Recommendation: ★★☆☆☆ (30/100) - 该论文主要测试替代暗物质的星系动力学框架（CCC、MOND、NFW），属于星系动力学与暗物质模型比较研究，与用户主要关注的强引力透镜（尤其是星系-星系强透镜）及高红移星系动力学、分子气体、尘埃等主题仅有间接关联。论文未涉及引力透镜方法或数据，也未包含用户核心作者。虽然涉及暗物质和星系旋转曲线，但并非用户主要兴趣领域，且未使用透镜作为研究工具。

TL;DR: 本文对协变耦合常数（CCC）框架进行了全面检验，使用175个盘状星系（SPARC样本）将其与MOND和NFW暗物质晕模型进行对比。研究发现，一个参数的平滑CCC模型在拟合旋转曲线方面与逐星系拟合的MOND相当（平均χ²ν为2.58对2.65），优于NFW模型。CCC的截止密度虽不具普适性，但重新表示为加速度后，其散布与MOND的a₀相当，量级约为2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²。

Motivation: CCC框架最初是为了解释JWST高红移观测而提出的，它包含一个与局部密度相关的协变常数有效质量场机制，可以在没有粒子暗物质的情况下修改星系动力学。此前仅对少量天体进行了测试，本研究旨在将其应用于完整的SPARC样本进行全面评估。

Method: 采用逆问题框架，每个模型从观测到的旋转曲线预测重子旋转曲线。改进早期研究中的尖锐密度截止（被证明是非物理的），替换为平滑过渡——这是密度空间中MOND插值函数的类比，不引入新参数。在相同基础上比较平滑CCC、MOND以及单参数和双参数NFW模型，使用约化χ²ν作为评价指标。

Result: 单参数平滑CCC在56%的星系中给出更低的χ²ν，平均χ²ν为2.58（MOND为2.65），配对差异不显著。双参数NFW的拟合质量分布更广，存在更大的较差拟合或边界受限拟合尾部（平均χ²ν≈7）。CCC截止密度散布为0.82 dex，与星系大小相关；但重新表示为加速度a_t后，散布降至0.33 dex（与MOND a₀的0.34 dex相当），量级约2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²，且消除了尺寸相关性。

Conclusion: 尽管CCC框架并非为星系动力学设计，但它在描述旋转曲线方面与逐星系拟合的MOND表现相当。将截止密度重新解释为加速度后，其与MOND特征标度a₀的惊人一致性暗示了更深层的物理联系。

Abstract: The Covarying Coupling Constants (CCC) framework, developed to account for high-redshift JWST observations, contains a mechanism -- a covarying-constant effective mass field keyed to local density -- that modifies galactic dynamics without particle dark matter. We test it against the full Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves (SPARC) sample of 175 disc galaxies, extending an earlier study of a few objects. Working in an inverse formulation, in which each model predicts the baryonic rotation curve from the observed one, we compare CCC against Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND) and one- and two-parameter Navarro-Frenk-White (NFW) haloes on identical footing, using the reduced $χ^2_ν$. We show that the published sharp density turn-off in the earlier study is unphysical and replace it with a smooth transition -- the density-space analogue of the MOND interpolating function, introducing no new parameter.
