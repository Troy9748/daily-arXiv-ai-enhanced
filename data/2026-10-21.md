Abstract: We present a technique using integral-field spectroscopy to kinematically isolate intracluster light (ICL) from the brightest cluster galaxy (BCG), enabling insights into their contrasting stellar populations and the inner dark matter halo profile. Simulations indicate that the inner halo profile varies systematically for different dark matter models. Applied to larger samples, our method therefore offers a complementary route to testing the nature of dark matter, while simultaneously revealing the stellar origins of the ICL.
