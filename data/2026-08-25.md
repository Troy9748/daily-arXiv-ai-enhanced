TL;DR: 首次将弱透镜峰陡度统计应用于HSC Y1数据，结合峰高统计得到S8与低浓度参数，支持强重子反馈。

Motivation: 弱透镜峰陡度统计对晕密度轮廓敏感，而密度轮廓编码了重子反馈和暗物质性质的关键信息，因此有望利用弱透镜数据对这些效应进行统计约束。

Abstract: As a new probe, the weak lensing (WL) peak steepness statistics is sensitive to the density profile of halos that encodes important information of baryonic feedback and dark matter properties, leading to a promising means to statistically constrain these effects using WL data. Within the phenomenological framework of HMcode2016 that attributes the baryonic feedback solely to the reduction of the halo concentration parameter and focusing on high peaks originated dominantly from massive clusters, our analyses by combining WL peak height and steepness statistics resulted in $S_8=0.76^{+0.08}_{-0.07}$ with the maximum-a-posteriori (MAP) of $0.79$ and low concentrations. The result tends to support phenomenologically strong baryonic feedback effects at cluster scales.

### [2] [Assessing the large-scale angular clustering of UNIONS Lyman Break Galaxies via cross-correlations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09943)
*Constantin Payerne,Christophe Yèche,William d'Assignies Doumerg,Hendrik Hildebrandt,Martin Kilbinger,Calum Murray,Thomas de Boer,Kenneth C. Chambers,Scott Chapman,Michael J. Hudson,Alan W. McConnachie*

Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆ (18/100) - 该文属于高红移星系的大尺度角聚类与宇宙学互相关研究，虽涉及CMB透镜，但核心是宇宙学探针与成像系统性，而非星系-星系强引力透镜或高红移星系动力学/多相气体，与用户主要兴趣关联很弱。

TL;DR: 本文评估了用UNIONS巡天选出的莱曼断裂星系（LBG）作为高红移宇宙学探针的可行性，发现自角功率谱受成像系统性影响严重，但互相关测量则稳健可靠。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LBG是研究z>2大尺度结构的强有力示踪物，作者希望检验能否用UNIONS多波段测光星表选出的LBG，通过两点统计量对高红移宇宙进行宇宙学探测。

Method: 先尝试测量LBG自角功率谱，并用线性与非线性方法校正成像系统性；随后转向与外部示踪物的互相关测量，包括Planck CMB透镜收敛场以及DESI DR1和Quaia的类星体，并结合真实数据与模拟星表进行验证。

Conclusion: UNIONS选出的LBG可作为z~2.5处互相关宇宙学的可靠示踪物，互相关技术是在复杂成像系统性下从测光高红移星系样本中提取宇宙学信息的强有力且稳健的途径。

Abstract: Lyman-break galaxies (LBGs), selected via the strong spectral break blueward of the Lyman limit, are powerful tracers of large-scale structure at redshifts $z>2$. In this work, we assess the feasibility of using LBGs selected from the Ultraviolet Near Infrared Optical Northern Survey (UNIONS) multi-band photometric catalog as cosmological probes of the high-redshift Universe using two-point statistics. We demonstrate that spatially varying imaging systematics, driven by variations in PSF depth, seeing across the UNIONS footprint, limit robust measurements of the LBG auto-angular power spectrum on large scales, even after correcting the LBG field with linear or non-linear mitigation techniques. This study shows that clustering analyses of faint galaxy samples close to survey depth are challenging. We therefore turn to cross-correlation measurements with external tracers, in particular the \textit{Planck} CMB lensing convergence and quasars from DESI DR1 and \textit{Quaia}, which are less sensitive to the angular imaging systematics. Using both data and mock catalogues, we demonstrate that the LBG--CMB lensing cross-power spectrum can be measured more robustly than the auto-spectrum, with an amplitude consistent with theoretical predictions. Residual systematics primarily manifest as excess variance at large angular scales, without introducing a significant bias in the recovered signal. Taken together, these results establish UNIONS-selected LBGs as reliable tracers for cross-correlation cosmology at $z\sim 2.5$, and highlight cross-correlation techniques as a powerful and robust avenue for extracting cosmological information from photometric high-redshift galaxy samples in the presence of complex imaging systematics.

Motivation: RCW 103是一个伽马射线超新星遗迹，其宇宙线加速效率及激波能量分配机制尚不清楚，需要通过观测其周围星际分子和原子气体的分布与运动学来约束激波与星际介质的相互作用及宇宙线加速过程。

Method: 使用Mopra的12CO(J=1-0)、ALMA的13CO(J=1-0)以及ATCA与Parkes的HI数据，分析RCW 103周围气体的速度分布、空间对应关系和位置-速度图，并据此估算膨胀速度与宇宙线质子能量。

Result: 在-58.7至-43.5 km/s速度范围内的CO云与RCW 103的X射线壳层在空间上明显对应，尤其从西北向东南延伸；位置-速度图显示膨胀速度约7.5 km/s；在假设星际质子密度约810 cm^-3下，估算总宇宙线质子能量约3.0(+1.1/-0.6)×10^47 erg，比同年龄的其他伽马射线超新星遗迹低一个数量级以上。

Conclusion: RCW 103内部仍存在大量致密气体且热X射线辐射占主导，说明相当一部分激波能量可能通过气体加热转化为热能，从而降低了宇宙线加速效率。

Abstract: We present a study of the interstellar molecular and atomic gas associated with the gamma-ray supernova remnant (SNR) RCW 103 using the Mopra $^{12}$CO($J$ = 1-0), Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array $^{13}$CO($J$ = 1-0), and the Australia Telescope Compact Array & Parkes HI data. We find that CO clouds in the velocity range from $-58.7$ to $-43.5$ km s$^{-1}$ show a clear spatial correspondence with the X-ray shell of RCW 103, particularly extending from the northwestern to the southeastern regions. The position-velocity diagram of the CO emission reveals an expanding gas motion with an expansion velocity of $ΔV \sim$$7.5$ km s$^{-1}$, which may have been produced by shock waves and/or stellar winds from the progenitor system. We estimate the total cosmic-ray proton energy to be $\sim$$3.0^{+1.1}_{-0.6} \times 10^{47}$ erg, adopting a total interstellar proton density of $\sim$$810$ cm$^{-3}$. This value is more than an order of magnitude lower than those inferred for other gamma-ray emitting SNRs of comparable age. Given that a substantial amount of dense gas remains within the interior of RCW 103, where thermal X-ray emission is dominant, our results possibly suggest that a significant fraction of the shock energy may have been converted into thermal energy via gas heating, thereby reducing the efficiency of cosmic-ray acceleration.
