Abstract: Offsets between cluster-scale dark matter halos and brightest cluster galaxies (BCGs) inferred from strong lensing (SL) models have been used to investigate cluster dynamics and dark matter physics. However, it is necessary to test whether these offsets are required by SL data themselves. We investigate whether allowing these offsets improves predictive accuracy and examine the effects on magnification, time-delay predictions, and critical curve positions. We evaluate lens models of Abell 370, RX J1347.5-1145, and MACS J0416.1-2403 using the jackknife method, which assesses predictions for source systems excluded from the lens reconstruction. Models are constructed with MrMARTIAN, which combines analytic mass profiles with a regularized grid that represents mass structure not captured by the profiles. We compare fixed and free halo position models across regularization weights. The root mean square values averaged over excluded systems agree within bootstrap uncertainties, while the median paired differences remain close to zero across all three clusters and tested regularization weights. These results indicate comparable predictive accuracy between the fixed and free models. The total mass distributions remain broadly similar, although the magnitudes of fitted halo offsets vary with regularization, showing that the mass decomposition is not unique. For these three clusters, offsets between BCGs and cluster-scale halos in the lens models are not required by the SL data to improve predictive accuracy within the MrMARTIAN framework. The mass decomposition between the regularized grid and cluster-scale halos is not uniquely determined. These findings limit physical interpretations based on fitted halo positions alone. The model dependence of magnification and time-delay estimation can also affect the inferred intrinsic properties of high-redshift sources or the Hubble constant.

### [2] [Toward Detecting the Moving Lens Effect with Optical Spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38457)
*David Wittman,Rodrigo Stancioli,Christopher Hopp*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Recommendation: ★★★★☆ (78/100) - 该论文直接研究强引力透镜的移动透镜效应，属于用户核心领域（强引力透镜）的观测可行性研究，使用强透镜多像光谱差异方法，与用户对强透镜的兴趣高度一致；但研究对象为星系团并合动力学，而非用户更侧重的高红移星系动力学、多相气体或暗物质子晕，且作者不在用户核心作者列表中，因此未达到最高分。

TL;DR: 本文研究了引力透镜天体的天空平面运动对透镜光子波长的移动效应，论证了利用强透镜多像光谱差异探测该效应的可行性，并探讨其对并合模型约束的作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 移动透镜效应比宇宙学与多普勒效应对同一光子的影响小几个数量级，但通过比较同一源的强透镜不同像可以分离出来。若透镜质量大、运动快（如落入另一星系团的星系团），透镜光谱波长差异可达10^-6量级，这为探测提供了可能性。

Method: 以并合星系团RM J001938.7+033557.3为案例，使用Keck/Deimos光学光谱数据评估探测该效应的可行性；同时在ΛCDM宇宙学下量化可能被识别为并合星系团的系统之预测天空平面运动，并研究天空平面运动信息如何帮助约束并合模型。

Result: 移动透镜效应的测量能够在以下两类模型之间进行区分：子团在第一次近心点后向外运动的模型，与子团在第一次远心点后回到相同间距的模型。

Conclusion: 测量移动透镜效应可有效消歧并合模型，为利用强透镜光谱探测星系团并合动力学提供了新途径。

Abstract: Relativity predicts that sky-plane motion of a gravitational lens changes the wavelengths of lensed photons. This moving lens effect is orders of magnitude smaller than cosmological and Doppler effects on the same photons, but can be isolated by comparing different strongly lensed images of a given source. With a massive, fast-moving lens such as a galaxy cluster falling into another cluster, lensed spectra of a given source could differ in wavelength by up to 1 part in $10^6$. We examine the feasibility of detecting this effect with optical spectroscopy, using Keck/Deimos data on the merging cluster RM J001938.7+033557.3 as a case study. We also quantify the predicted sky-plane motions of systems likely to be identified as merging clusters in a $Λ$CDM cosmology, and investigate how information on sky-plane motions could help constrain merger models. We find that measurement of the moving lens effect would disambiguate between models in which the subclusters are outbound after first pericenter and those in which the subclusters have returned to the same separation after first apocenter.

Abstract Translation:
相对论预言，引力透镜在天空平面上的运动会使被透镜化的光子波长发生变化。这种移动透镜效应比同一批光子受到的宇宙学效应和多普勒效应小几个数量级，但可以通过比较给定源的不同强透镜像来分离出来。对于一个质量大且快速运动的透镜，例如一个正在落入另一个星系团的星系团，给定源的透镜化光谱在波长上可能相差最多 $10^6$ 分之一。我们以合并星系团 RM J001938.7+033557.3 的 Keck/Deimos 数据为案例，研究用光学光谱探测这种效应的可行性。我们还量化了在 $\Lambda$CDM 宇宙学中可能被识别为合并星系团的系统的预测天空平面运动，并探讨关于天空平面运动的信息如何帮助约束合并模型。我们发现，测量移动透镜效应将能够区分以下两类模型：子星系团在第一次近心点之后向外运动的模型，以及子星系团在第一次远心点之后回到相同分离距离的模型。

Conclusion Translation:

Abstract: We present a new measurement of the stellar and dark matter distributions in massive early-type galaxies at redshift $0.4<z<0.6$ by combining stacked strong and weak lensing measurements. The stacked weak lensing measurements down to the small radius of $0.013\,\mathrm{Mpc}/h$ enabled by the Subaru Telescope Hyper Suprime-Cam data are combined with stacked enclosed projected mass measurements from 13 strong lens systems. We find that adding strong lensing constraints improves constraints on the stellar and dark matter distributions. The central dark matter profile is found to be consistent with the standard Navarro-Frenk-White density profile. The stellar initial mass function is found to be bottom-heavy. The total density profile in the inner region is described well by the power-law density profile with the slope of $-1.96\pm 0.07$, which can also explain weak lensing signals out to $1\,\mathrm{Mpc}/h$ reasonably well. The stacked weak lensing profile constrains an internal mass-sheet transformation parameter to $λ_{\mathrm{c}}=0.978\pm0.010$. Our analysis demonstrates that the combination of the stacked strong and weak lensing serves as a powerful tool for studying central density profiles of galaxies.

Abstract: Primordial magnetic fields (PMFs), potentially generated during cosmic inflation or early-universe phase transitions, can modify cosmic structure formation by enhancing the post-recombination matter power spectrum. In this work, we investigate the impact of PMFs on galaxy formation using a suite of high-resolution cosmological hydrodynamical simulations evolved to $z=0$. Our simulations are initialized with PMF-induced enhancements to the matter power spectrum and include more comprehensive baryonic subgrid models than previous work, incorporating both stellar and AGN feedback. We find that PMFs systematically accelerate early structure formation, producing more abundant halos and galaxies, higher baryon fractions, enhanced star formation rates, and rapid growth of supermassive black holes at high redshifts ($z \sim 10$). However, the resulting enhanced stellar and AGN feedback efficiently processes and expels gas, reducing the late-time differences between PMF and standard $Λ$CDM models. The PMF signatures are therefore strongest at high redshift and gradually weaken toward low redshift as nonlinear evolution and baryonic feedback dominate over the initial PMF-induced enhancements. The impact of PMFs depends strongly on the characteristic peak scale, $k_{\rm peak}$, of the initial power enhancement, with models yielding similar non-linear evolution if they share a similar $k_{\rm peak}$, even when other PMF parameters differ. Our results highlight the critical role of comprehensive baryonic physics in accurately quantifying PMF signatures.

Abstract Translation:
原初磁场（PMFs）可能在宇宙暴胀或早期宇宙相变期间产生，通过增强复合后的物质功率谱来改变宇宙结构形成。在本工作中，我们利用一套演化至 $z=0$ 的高分辨率宇宙学流体动力学模拟，研究了PMFs对星系形成的影响。我们的模拟初始条件包含了PMF引起的物质功率谱增强，并采用了比以往工作更全面的重子亚网格模型，同时考虑了恒星和AGN反馈。我们发现，PMFs系统性地加速了早期结构形成，在高红移（$z \sim 10$）处产生了更丰富的晕和星系、更高的重子分数、增强的恒星形成率以及超大质量黑洞的快速生长。然而，由此增强的恒星和AGN反馈有效地处理并驱离了气体，减少了PMF与标准 $Λ$CDM模型在晚期的差异。因此，PMF特征在高红移处最强，并随着非线性演化和重子反馈主导超过初始PMF引起的增强而逐渐减弱至低红移。PMFs的影响强烈依赖于初始功率增强的特征峰尺度 $k_{\rm peak}$，即使其他PMF参数不同，只要具有相似的 $k_{\rm peak}$，模型就会产生相似的非线性演化。我们的结果凸显了全面的重子物理在准确量化PMF特征中的关键作用。

Conclusion Translation:
基于可用的摘要，全面的重子物理在准确量化PMF印记中起关键作用；PMF信号主要出现在高红移，低红移时被非线性演化和重子反馈掩盖。
