### [3] [Molecular hydrogen formation on dust: The impact of gas-dust drift on formation efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07178)
*Stefan Reissl,Simon C. O. Glover,Ralf S. Klessen,Liam S. Morrissey,Mordecai-Mark Mac Low*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

Recommendation: ★★☆☆☆ (38/100) - 该文研究尘埃表面H₂形成的微观物理（气-尘漂移、粘附概率、LH/ER反应），属于星际介质化学与尘埃物理的数值模拟工作。虽与画像中的“dust”“molecular”关键词有字面重叠，但既非强引力透镜，也不涉及高红移星系动力学、运动学、偏振或多相气体观测，与用户核心研究方向关联薄弱。

TL;DR: 本文研究气体-尘埃漂移对星际介质中H₂形成的影响，重点考察漂移增强的H原子碰撞率与高撞击能量下粘附概率降低之间的竞争。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: H₂主要在尘埃颗粒表面形成，尘埃温度与表面性质对H₂形成的影响已被充分研究，但气体-尘埃漂移的作用很少受到关注。在动力学活跃的环境中相对气体-尘埃运动会显著出现，因此需要理解漂移如何改变H₂形成。

Method: 采用事件驱动的动力学蒙特卡洛模型，追踪球形硅酸盐和碳质颗粒上的单个H原子，包括吸附、表面迁移、热脱附以及Langmuir-Hinshelwood（LH）和Eley-Rideal（ER）反应。漂移用平移的麦克斯韦速度分布描述，并比较恒定粘附概率与依赖撞击能量的粘附概率。

Result: 漂移导致吸附H原子和H₂形成在颗粒表面分布愈发各向异性。假设恒定粘附时，漂移通过提高碰撞率增强H₂形成，效率可达ε=0.3-0.4；采用能量依赖粘附时，强漂移反而抑制两种材料上的形成，效率降至ε=0.01-0.03。碳质颗粒在更高尘埃温度下仍保持高效。ER反应在多数参数空间占主导，并在强漂移下因表面种群减少抑制LH反应而变得更重要。

Conclusion: 气体-尘埃漂移下增强的碰撞率并不必然提高H₂形成。模拟动力学活跃环境的模型应同时考虑相对气体-尘埃速度及其对粘附的影响。

Abstract: Molecular hydrogen is predominantly formed on dust-grain surfaces in the interstellar medium, where relative gas-dust motion can arise in dynamically active environments. While the dependence of H$_2$ formation on grain temperature and surface properties is well studied, the impact of gas-dust drift has received little attention. We investigate how gas-dust drift modifies H$_2$ formation, focusing on the competition between the drift-enhanced H-atom collision rate and reduced sticking at higher impact energies. We use an event-driven kinetic Monte Carlo model that follows individual H atoms on spherical silicate and carbonaceous grains, including adsorption, surface migration, thermal desorption, and Langmuir-Hinshelwood (LH) and Eley-Rideal (ER) reactions. Drift is described by a shifted Maxwellian velocity distribution, and we compare constant and impact-energy-dependent sticking probabilities. Drift produces increasingly anisotropic distributions of adsorbed H and H$_2$ formation across the grain surface. Assuming constant sticking, increasing drift enhances H$_2$ formation through the higher collision rate, with efficiencies up to $ε=0.3-0.4$. With energy-dependent sticking, strong drift instead suppresses formation on both materials, reducing efficiencies to $ε=0.01-0.03$. Carbonaceous grains remain efficient to higher dust temperatures than silicate grains. ER reactions dominate over most of the investigated parameter space and become increasingly important at strong drift as the reduced surface population suppresses LH reactions. Thus, enhanced collision rates under gas-dust drift do not necessarily increase H$_2$ formation.
