### [1] [Semi-Analytic Predictions for Strong-Lensing Perturbers in Cold and Self-Interacting Dark Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31881)
*Jonathan Tekverk,Duncan K. Adams,Simon Birrer,Rouven Essig,Daniel Gilman*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Motivation: 强引力透镜对暗物质晕的丰度与内部结构敏感，可作为自相互作用暗物质（SIDM）中引力热演化的探针。作者希望定量回答：在给定暗物质模型下，子晕"多常"能重现强透镜成象中推断出的单个扰动体，从而区分CDM与SIDM。

Method: 将半解析CDM子晕形成历史与SIDM引力热演化模型组合，在SIDM参数空间中生成CDM与SIDM子晕群体；计算其投影包围质量与基于偏折的投影密度斜率，并与引力成像推断的三个扰动体（PJ011646、SDSS J0946+1006、JVAS B1938+666）比较，统计每个主晕中相匹配子晕的出现率（含核化与核塌缩两类）。

Result: 在σ0/mχ=147.1 cm²/g、w=120 km/s下：PJ011646约0.6个核化SIDM子晕/主晕（CDM约0.002），核塌缩者过陡；SDSS J0946+1006约3个核塌缩、约0.004个核化（900个CDM实现中为零），但重子效应可能对其大质量子晕有影响；JVAS B1938+666的"detection V"在2σ后验区内约220个核塌缩与20个核化（CDM约0.2），CDM候选需异常早的吸积（中位红移z~13）。同时重现三者需w≳10² km/s（使~5.6×10¹⁰ M⊙的SDSS J0946+1006前身天体得以塌缩），并在所研究参数集中要求σ0/mχ≳10² cm²/g。

Conclusion: 半解析模型可定量评估不同暗物质模型下子晕重现单个透镜扰动体的频率；该模块化框架可随潮汐剥离与晚期引力热演化模型的改进而更新。重子效应与更大参数空间扫描是后续需要检验的方向。

Abstract: Strong gravitational lensing is sensitive to the abundance and internal structure of dark matter halos, providing a probe of gravothermal evolution in self-interacting dark matter (SIDM). We combine semi-analytic cold dark matter (CDM) subhalo histories with a model for gravothermal evolution in SIDM to generate CDM and SIDM subhalo populations across SIDM parameter space. We predict their projected enclosed masses and deflection-based projected density slopes and compare them with three perturbers inferred through gravitational imaging in PJ011646, SDSS J0946+1006, and JVAS B1938+666. For SIDM model parameters $σ_0/m_χ=147.1$ cm$^2$/g and $w=120$ km/s, SIDM subhalos reproduce all three perturbers more often than CDM subhalos. For PJ011646, we find ~0.6 cored SIDM subhalos per host matching the perturber, compared with ~0.002 in CDM, while core-collapsed subhalos are too steep. For SDSS J0946+1006, we find ~3 core-collapsed and ~0.004 cored SIDM subhalos per host, compared with none in 900 CDM realizations, although baryons, neglected here, may matter for such massive subhalos. For "detection V" in JVAS B1938+666, we find ~220 core-collapsed and ~20 cored SIDM subhalos per host within the $2σ$ posterior region, compared with ~0.2 in CDM; the CDM candidates require exceptionally early accretion, at median redshift $z\sim13$. Simultaneously reproducing all three perturbers requires $w\gtrsim10^2$ km/s, so that the massive (~$5.6\times10^{10}M_\odot$) progenitors of SDSS J0946+1006 candidates can collapse, and is achieved for $σ_0/m_χ\gtrsim10^2$ cm$^2$/g in the parameter sets studied. Our results show how semi-analytic models can quantify how often subhalos reproduce individual perturbers in different dark matter models, and our modular framework can be readily updated as models of tidal stripping and late-stage gravothermal evolution in SIDM improve. (abridged)

Abstract: The interplay between star formation and the interstellar medium (ISM) gives rise to a feedback cycle that is one of the key drivers of galaxy evolution. The coldest gas in the ISM provides the fuel for star formation, and, in turn, the ISM is affected by feedback in the form of photons, shocks, and cosmic rays. Radio recombination lines (RRLs) offer a unique way of characterizing the gas in the ISM as extinction free probes of the gas kinematics, density, temperature, and radiation field. Hydrogen RRLs (HRRLs) probe gas where hydrogen is ionized, while carbon RRLs (CRRLs) probe cold gas in the HI-to-H$_{2}$ transition layers. The superb sensitivity, spatial resolution, large field of view, and frequency resolution and coverage of the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) will allow for efficient observations of RRLs, that will make significant impacts to the study of the diffuse ISM. By observing RRLs with the SKA we will be able to determine the thermal and turbulent balance, chemical enrichment, sizes, and ionization rate of the cold and ionized gases from degree-scales down to arcsecond scales in our Galaxy.

恒星形成与星际介质（ISM）之间的相互作用产生了一个反馈循环，该循环是星系演化的关键驱动因素之一。ISM中最冷的气体为恒星形成提供了燃料，反过来，ISM又受到光子、激波和宇宙射线形式的反馈影响。射电复合线（RRLs）提供了一种独特的表征ISM气体的方式，作为无消光探针来探测气体运动学、密度、温度和辐射场。氢射电复合线（HRRLs）探测氢被电离的气体，而碳射电复合线（CRRLs）探测HI到H$_{2}$过渡层中的冷气体。平方公里阵列（SKA）的卓越灵敏度、空间分辨率、大视场以及频率分辨率和覆盖范围将允许高效观测RRLs，这将对弥漫ISM的研究产生重大影响。通过用SKA观测RRLs，我们将能够确定银河系中从度尺度到角秒尺度的冷气体和电离气体的热与湍流平衡、化学富集、尺寸和电离率。
