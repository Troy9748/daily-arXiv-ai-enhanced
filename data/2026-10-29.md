Abstract: The circumgalactic medium (CGM) of massive high-redshift galaxies contains inflowing filamentary gas, recycled material, and feedback-driven outflows. We investigate the formation and evolution of dense CGM clumps in two cosmological zoom-in simulations of the same massive halo at $z > 6$: the fiducial Fable model and the modified Fable-SE model, which includes enhanced early black hole growth through mild super-Eddington accretion. We identify dense gas clumps in the CGM and follow their gas properties, stellar content, and radial evolution across cosmic time. At the resolution probed here, the two simulations show broadly similar distributions of clump gas mass, gas size and associated stellar mass. Instead, the clump populations differ mainly in their abundance, metallicity, and radial evolution. Fable-SE produces substantially more metal-enriched clumps, including an outward-moving population during the strong AGN-driven blow-out phase at $z \lesssim 7$. These systems are associated with disturbed HI-rich gas and relatively high gas densities, linking the increased prevalence of dense, metal-enriched CGM structures in Fable-SE to its stronger AGN-driven blow-out phase. Although inward migration dominates overall, Fable-SE also contains an outward-moving clump population during the blow-out phase, with some clumps moving beyond the virial radius. Our results therefore indicate that the distribution of dense CGM clumps around massive high-redshift galaxies reflects both filamentary accretion and feedback-driven CGM evolution, with strong early AGN activity associated with the outward migration of dense, metal-enriched gas structures containing relatively cool gas.

*Olga Borodina,Jake S. Bennett,Yueying Ni,Rainer Weinberger,Michaela Hirschmann,Boon Kiat Oh,Lars Hernquist*

Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆ (22/100) - 该文为理想化湍流盒数值模拟，研究AGN喷流与星际介质相互作用及反馈，与用户核心的强引力透镜、暗物质子晕、高红移星系动力学/多相气体观测均无直接关联；仅作者Michaela Hirschmann与画像有弱匹配，但主题偏离明显。

Motivation: 活动星系核喷流被认为是星系演化的重要驱动者，但喷流如何把能量耦合到星际介质仍不清楚；近期在不同形态星系中观测到约kpc尺度的喷流，亟需理解喷流—星际介质相互作用与反馈如何塑造喷流演化。

Abstract: Active galactic nuclei (AGN) are thought to be important drivers of galaxy evolution. However, the way AGN jets couple their energy to the interstellar medium (ISM) remains poorly understood. Recent observations have revealed $\sim$kpc-scale jets in galaxies of different morphological types. Here, we use idealized turbulent box simulations with accreting black holes to investigate how jet-ISM interactions and feedback shape the evolution of jets. We show that a turbulent ISM is a key ingredient in both jet launching and jet propagation. Without turbulence, jet feedback initially shuts off the cold gas supply to the black hole and the system enters a steady state with jet power $\leq 10^{39}\,\mathrm{erg\,s^{-1}}$ for a $10^{6}\,\mathrm{M}_\odot$ black hole. In a turbulent medium, jets fill voids with hot diffuse material which the turbulence then carries away, replenishing the black hole's fuel supply. However, above a critical jet efficiency factor, the jet begins to significantly deplete its fuel supply and enters a self-limiting accretion regime. This critical factor depends on the ISM Mach number, with lower turbulence yielding a lower threshold. Moreover, while the jet power depends on the ambient gas density in the vicinity of the black hole, we find that, at fixed turbulent structure, the morphology and extent of jet propagation are largely insensitive to the average background density; turbulence in the ISM dominates over mean density in setting how far jets propagate, although denser media experience higher total energy injection and stronger heating.

Motivation: 弱透镜宇宙学分析依赖对层析红移分布 $N(z)$ 的精确标定，需要评估不同机器学习方法（尤其是能输出完整后验 PDF 的生成模型）在重建 $N(z)$ 时的优劣。

Method: 使用 DESI DR1 光谱数据（星系与类星体目标）与 Legacy Surveys DR10 测光数据，在 $0<z_{\rm spec}<4.5$ 范围内以 110 万源训练、200 万源（不重叠天区）测试；对比条件归一化流、随机森林与分位数随机森林。除点估计指标外，引入 Wasserstein 距离与 JS 散度等分布级指标评估堆叠 PDF 的层析重建精度，并基于独立验证样本进行红移相关的事后 PDF 校准。

Result: 点估计方面，NF 在 $0<z_{\rm spec}<1.6$ 取得最低散射与异常值比例，在 $z_{\rm spec}>1.6$ 三项指标全面优于两个基线。分布层面，堆叠 NF 后验 PDF 的层析 $N(z)$ 重建优于 QRF，高红移尤甚：$z_{\rm median}>2.5$ 时 NF 的平均红移偏差为 $0.060\pm0.031$（QRF 为 0.318），概率重叠为 $0.905\pm0.008$（QRF 为 0.787）。校准后，$0.2<z_{\rm median}<1.0$ 的四个 bin 平均红移偏差绝对值为 0.0016–0.0019，优于基准 $0.002(1+z)$；最高红移 bin 偏差降至 $0.016\pm0.011$。

Conclusion: NF 的 PDF 输出在层析 $N(z)$ 重建中具有显著潜力，结合事后校准可达到高精度；但跨星系族群迁移模型仍存在挑战。

Abstract: Accurate calibration of tomographic redshift distributions, $N(z)$ is essential for cosmological analysis of weak lensing data. We investigate conditional normalizing flows (NFs) and compare their performance with random forest (RF) and quantile random forest (QRF) models. We consider DESI galaxy and quasar targets from DESI DR1 spectroscopic data and with the photometry from Legacy Surveys DR10. We train on 1.1 million sources and test on 2.0 million sources in a non-overlapping sky region ($0<z_{\rm spec}<4.5$). For point estimation, the NF achieves the lowest scatter and outlier fraction at $0<z_{\rm spec}<1.6$ and outperforms both baselines across all three metrics at $z_{\rm spec}>1.6$. By introducing distribution-level metrics like Wasserstein distance and JS divergence, we find that stacking NF posterior PDFs provides more accurate tomographic $N(z)$ reconstruction than QRF, particularly at high redshifts. For $z_{\rm median}>2.5$, the NF achieves a smaller absolute mean-redshift bias ($0.060\pm0.031$ versus $0.318$) and higher probability overlap ($0.905\pm0.008$ versus $0.787$) than QRF. We apply redshift-dependent post-hoc PDF calibration derived from a held-out validation sample. After calibration, the absolute mean-redshift biases in four bins spanning $0.2<z_{\rm median}<1.0$ range from $0.0016$ to $0.0019$, with central values below referring benchmark $0.002(1+z)$. In the highest-redshift bin, calibration further reduces the bias to $0.016\pm0.011$. Our results demonstrate the potential of NF PDFs for tomographic $N(z)$ reconstruction while highlighting challenges in transferring models across galaxy populations.
