Abstract: Galaxies with extreme amounts of neutral atomic hydrogen (HI), known as HI-eXtreme (HIX) galaxies, paradoxically exhibit normal or low star formation rates. To investigate the bottleneck in their star formation, we present ALMA CO(1-0) observations for five HIX survey galaxies: ESO208-G026, ESO378-G003, ESO381-G005, ESO417-G018, and ESO055-G013. While these systems possess massive, extended HI discs, their molecular gas discs are compact with normal H$_{2}$ masses relative to their stellar mass. Dynamical modelling with 3DBarolo reveals that the HI and molecular gas are kinematically consistent. Both gas phases show high velocity dispersions, indicating enhanced turbulence compared to regular late-type galaxies. We calculate the specific angular momentum for the stellar, atomic, and molecular components alongside the global stability parameter ($q$). Most of the system angular momentum is stored in the HI discs, rendering them globally stable against collapse. This stability suppresses the atomic-to-molecular gas conversion, as evidenced by extremely long HI depletion times contrasted with typical H$_{2}$ depletion times. The high angular momentum and HI excess likely originate from past gas accretion or recent tidal interactions; this is especially apparent in ESO378-G003 and ESO381-G005, which exhibit extended HI structures and positive tidal indices.

*L. Arriscado,I. Shivaei,L. A. Boogaard,A. Mizener,D. Calzetti,P. G. Pérez-González,F. Rodríguez Montero,G. H. Rieke,Y. Zhu,A. Battisti,A. J. Bunker,E. Curtis-Lake,T. Díaz-Santos,Z. Ji,P. Rinaldi,B. Rodríguez del Pino,S. Tacchella,A. Varo-O'Ferrall,C. C. Williams,J. Witstok*

Recommendation: ★★★☆☆ (58/100) - 论文聚焦宇宙正午（1.5<z<2.7）星系的尘埃衰减曲线、2175Å UV凸起与PAH发射，直接命中画像中的 cosmic noon、dust、galaxy attenuation 等主题，且使用JWST/MUSE多波段观测，与用户对高红移星系多相气体与尘埃的兴趣部分契合；但核心科学问题是尘埃/PAH物理与金属丰度关系，不涉及强引力透镜、暗物质子晕或高红移气体动力学，方法上也无透镜建模，故只能给中等分数。

TL;DR: 研究49个宇宙正午星系，首次系统证实UV 2175Å吸收凸起幅度与PAH质量分数正相关，并表明恒星质量相关性实为金属丰度依赖。

Motivation: 2175Å紫外衰减凸起是星系衰减曲线中最显著却最缺乏理解的特征之一，其主要候选载体PAH分子在高金属丰度环境中更普遍，但此前缺乏对PAH丰度与凸起关系在宇宙正午时期的系统观测检验。

Abstract: The UV 2175 Å attenuation bump is one of the most prominent yet poorly understood features of galaxy attenuation curves. Its main candidate carrier is polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon (PAH) molecules, which are more prevalent in high-metallicity environments. We present the first systematic study of the relationship between PAH abundance and the UV bump in 49 galaxies at cosmic noon (1.5 < z < 2.7), examining its dependence on stellar mass, star formation rate (SFR), and V-band optical depth. For 19 galaxies, we also investigate gas-phase metallicity using oxygen abundances. We measure the bump amplitude from VLT/MUSE spectroscopy and derive galaxy properties, including the PAH-to-dust mass fraction $q_{\rm PAH}$, through spectral energy distribution fitting with Prospector, combining JWST/NIRCam and JWST/MIRI photometry. Oxygen abundances are derived from strong-line diagnostics using Keck/MOSFIRE and JWST/NIRSpec data. We find a positive correlation between bump amplitude $E_{\rm bump}$ and $q_{\rm PAH}$ (Spearman $ρ= 0.60$). UV bumps are rarely detected at low $q_{\rm PAH}$, supporting PAHs as their primary carriers. Both $E_{\rm bump}$ and $q_{\rm PAH}$ correlate with stellar mass, oxygen abundance, and optical depth, and anticorrelate with specific SFR, suggesting that PAHs and the UV bump are suppressed in low-metallicity, dust-poor, and/or intensely star-forming environments. This is consistent with PAH destruction in environments with limited dust shielding and/or strong radiation fields. A principal component analysis of $E_{\rm bump}$, $q_{\rm PAH}$, optical depth, specific SFR, and oxygen abundance explains 97% of the variance, suggesting that previously reported correlations with stellar mass reflect an underlying dependence on metallicity. Finally, variations in dust geometry may explain the wide range of bump amplitudes (0-0.5 mag) at high $q_{\rm PAH}$.

Abstract: The existence of primordial black holes (PBHs) could have significant effects on the formation of cosmic structures through both the seed effect and the Poisson effect. For an extended PBH mass function, this should lead to dark virialised bound clusters of black holes with modest mass, together with central black holes of larger mass. We consider the combination of three effects: the inevitable gravitational binding of small-scale inhomogeneities due to Poisson and seed fluctuations; the dilution through dynamical heating of all clusters smaller than around $10^{6}\,M_{\odot}$; the survival of clusters in the outer Galactic halo (i.e. towards the Magellanic Clouds) but their destruction in the central halo region through tidal disruption and collisions. We examine the implications of this for the constraints on the fraction of dark matter in PBHs from microlensing surveys and gravitational-wave events. Although the clustering of PBHs with the expected mass and radius is unlikely to relax the microlensing limits, the decline of the Galactic rotation curve might do so, allowing much of the dark matter to be in solar-mass PBHs formed at the quantum chromodynamics transition. We also emphasise that binary merger rates must be revised: binaries formed in PBH clusters can dominate the merger rate, which may be compatible with the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA gravitational-wave limits if PBHs provide the dark matter. More positively, Ultra-Faint Dwarf Galaxies and Little Red Dots might arise through a combination of the seed and Poisson effects. Our conclusions could be modified for PBHs produced from primordial fluctuations with exponential non-Gaussian tails, since the clustering would then exceed the Poissonian prediction, opening up new possibilities to explain dark matter with solar-mass PBHs.

### [5] [Magnetic monopoles produced by first-order phase transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29349)
*Yuefeng Di,Ligong Bian,Rong-Gen Cai*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆ (18/100) - 该文属粒子物理/早期宇宙方向，研究一阶相变产生的磁单极子、原初磁场与引力波，虽涉及 magnetic fields 与 dark matter 关键词，但均为宇宙学/粒子物理语境，与用户核心的强引力透镜、高红移星系动力学/多相气体/偏振等主题无实质交集。

Motivation: 磁单极子可能在一阶相变中产生，其残余丰度、伴随产生的磁场与引力波信号缺乏定量模拟；需要确定单极子能否解释原初宇宙磁场，并约束其质量。

Method: 采用Press-Ryden-Spergel方法进行首个三维格点模拟，覆盖相变后的湮灭阶段与膨胀宇宙中的标度（scaling）区进入过程；统计初始泡泡数与残余单极子丰度的定量关系，并计算单极子动力学源出的磁场和引力波功率谱。

Abstract: We present the first 3D lattice simulations of magnetic monopole production from a first-order phase transition, using the Press-Ryden-Spergel method, capturing the subsequent annihilation stage and the entry into the scaling regime in an expanding universe. We establish the quantitative relationship between the initial bubble number and the residual monopole abundance, and calculate the magnetic field and gravitational wave power spectra sourced by monopole dynamics. Crucially, we find that the magnetic field spectrum displays a steep $k^{-5}$ decline beyond the monopole size $w_M$, whereas the peak of the gravitational wave spectrum aligns with the scale of the mean monopole separation $d_M$, governed by $k^3$ and $k^{-3}$ scalings on the low- and high-wavenumber sides of the peak, respectively. However, since the resulting magnetic field strength is excluded by blazar observations, magnetic monopoles cannot be the origin of primordial cosmic magnetic fields. Finally, we determine the allowed monopole mass under the constraints of the Parker bound ($m_M\lesssim2.18\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{GeV}$) and dark matter abundance ($m_M\lesssim1.58\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{GeV}$).
