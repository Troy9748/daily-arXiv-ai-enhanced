### [1] [De-lensing cosmic birefringence with joint reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03761)
*Sebastian Belkner,Julien Carron*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Recommendation: ★★☆☆☆ (38/100) - 该文虽涉及lensing，但核心是CMB宇宙双折射与宇称破坏新物理的重建，属于宇宙学/粒子物理范畴，与用户主攻的星系-星系强引力透镜、高红移星系动力学与多相气体无实质交集。

TL;DR: 本文研究对CMB透镜场（梯度与旋度）和各向异性宇宙双折射在MAP（最大后验）点上的联合重建，其谱为近最优统计量；解析预测联合响应与噪声并在模拟上验证，展示幅度推断无偏，在SPT-3G+深度下误差相比二次估计量缩小5倍。

Motivation: 从CMB重建各向异性宇宙双折射受到透镜B模样本方差限制，且与透镜旋度部分简并，导致现有（二次估计量）方法精度受限。

Method: 在最大后验点上联合重建透镜（梯度与旋度）与双折射；解析推导联合响应与噪声，并用模拟数据验证；检验其幅度推断的无偏性。

Abstract: Reconstruction of anisotropic cosmic birefringence from the CMB is limited by lensing $B$-mode sample variance and partially degenerate with the lensing curl. We study the joint reconstruction of lensing (gradient and curl) and birefringence at the maximum-a-posteriori point, whose spectra are near-optimal statistics. We predict the joint response and noise analytically, validate them on simulations, and show unbiased inference of its amplitude. Errors will shrink $5$-fold relative to quadratic estimators at SPT-3G+ depths, promising much tighter constraints on parity-violating new physics, owing to the removal of the lensing $B$-polarization sample variance.

### [2] [Recovering Missing Degrees of Freedom in Simulation-Trained Field-Level Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02627)
*Masato Shirasaki,Shiro Ikeda*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆ (22/100) - 该文属于宇宙学弱透镜场级推断与生成模型方法学，仅与用户强引力透镜兴趣共享“lensing”泛关键词，科学问题、数据与方法均不对齐。

TL;DR: 该文将“方差收缩”视为缺失有效自由度的诊断指标，通过对连续归一化流的生成动力学施加物理启发的潜变量修正，在不增加训练仿真的情况下恢复了相当一部分缺失的变异性，并提升了弱透镜场级推断对分布外偏移的稳健性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 仿真训练的生成模型在宇宙学场级推断中很有前景，但因训练集有限，常出现方差收缩，即物理过程在实现层面的部分变异性未被学到的生成动力学捕获。作者希望把这一现象从“缺陷”转化为可诊断、可修正的信号。

Method: 将方差收缩解释为缺失有效自由度的诊断；在连续归一化流的生成动力学中引入物理启发的潜变量修正，无需额外训练仿真即可补回缺失的变异性；并以弱透镜场级推断为代表性场景，通过后验分析检验所学潜变量方向是否在推断中被积极使用。

Result: 潜变量修正恢复了相当比例的缺失变异性且不需额外仿真；后验分析显示所学潜变量方向在推断中被积极利用，并起到有效冗余参数的作用，吸收训练分布与目标分布之间的部分失配，使模型在场级推断中对分布外偏移更稳健。

Conclusion: 方差收缩可作为识别仿真训练生成模型中缺失自由度的实用诊断；通过物理启发的生成动力学修正可恢复这些自由度并提升稳健性，为相关方法提供了通用框架。

Abstract: Simulation-trained generative models are emerging as a powerful framework for field-level inference in cosmology, particularly in strongly nonlinear regimes where analytical approaches become intractable. However, the finite size of training datasets often leads to variance shrinkage, indicating that part of the realization-level variability of the underlying physical process is not captured by the learned generative dynamics. In this Letter, we interpret variance shrinkage as a diagnostic of missing effective degrees of freedom and introduce physics-informed latent modifications to the generative dynamics of a continuous normalizing flow. We show that these latent corrections recover a substantial fraction of the missing variability without requiring additional training simulations. Using weak-lensing field-level inference as a representative cosmological application, we further demonstrate through posterior analysis that the learned latent directions are actively exploited during inference and behave as effective nuisance parameters that absorb part of the mismatch between the training and target distributions. As a result, the model exhibits improved robustness to out-of-distribution shifts in field-level inference. Our work establishes variance shrinkage as a practical diagnostic for identifying missing degrees of freedom in simulation-trained generative models and provides a framework for recovering them through physics-informed modifications of generative dynamics.

Abstract Translation:
仿真训练的生成模型正逐渐成为宇宙学中场级推断的有力框架，尤其是在解析方法难以处理的强非线性区域。然而，训练数据集的有限规模往往导致方差收缩，这表明底层物理过程的部分实现级变异性未被学习到的生成动力学所捕获。在本快报中，我们将方差收缩解释为缺失有效自由度的诊断指标，并引入物理启发的潜变量修正来调整连续归一化流的生成动力学。我们表明，这些潜变量修正能够恢复相当一部分缺失的变异性，而无需额外的训练仿真。以弱透镜场级推断作为代表性宇宙学应用，我们进一步通过后验分析证明，学习到的潜变量方向在推断过程中被积极利用，并表现为有效的冗余参数，吸收训练分布与目标分布之间的部分失配。因此，该模型在场级推断中对分布外偏移表现出更好的稳健性。我们的工作将方差收缩确立为识别仿真训练生成模型中缺失自由度的实用诊断工具，并提供了一个通过物理启发的生成动力学修正来恢复这些自由度的框架。

Conclusion Translation:

Abstract: Strong gravitational lensing tightly constrains the projected mass within the Einstein aperture, but what else imaging alone can recover for edge-on disc lenses remains untested against known truth. Abstract: We present new IRAM 30 m observations of CO(1-0) and CO(2-1) emission in five Coma cluster galaxies undergoing ram pressure stripping. Molecular gas is detected in four galaxies, and CO emission is clearly detected in the stripped tails of three, extending up to $\sim50$ kpc from the stellar disks. The molecular gas masses in the tails range from a few $\times10^8\,M_\odot$ and account for up to 10-20% of the molecular gas remaining in the parent disks.
