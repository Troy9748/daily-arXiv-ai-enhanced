Abstract: We revisit a late-time, model-independent distance-consistency framework that combines strong gravitational lensing systems (SGLS), ultraviolet/X-ray quasars (QSOs), and 38 Sunyaev--Zel'dovich/X-ray galaxy-cluster angular-diameter distances. The analysis focuses on systematics rather than on presenting a competitive curvature measurement. Starting from the 158-system SGLS catalog, we exclude 30 systems that violate the lensing-geometric requirement $D_{A,ls}/D_{A,s}<1$ in the isothermal reference case $γ_{\rm lens}=2$, leaving a fixed 128-system SGLS parent sample for cross-matching with 1598 QSOs. A deterministic maximum-cardinality, minimum-mismatch one-to-one assignment yields 100 SGLS--QSO pairs at the nominal $Δd/d=5\%$ threshold. We propagate the measured X-ray flux uncertainties and the intrinsic QSO scatter, and, crucially, we include the full $38\times38$ covariance induced by using the same QSO distances in the kernel-smoothed cluster predictions. With $Ω_K\sim\mathcal{U}[-10,10]$, the joint posterior is $Ω_K=2.085^{+1.634}_{-1.103}$ (68\%; central 95\% interval $[0.269,6.028]$). We do not interpret the positive shift as evidence for an open Universe. \added{This positive posterior offset is driven by cross-probe calibration tensions, sample-assignment effects, and the cluster anchor; it cannot be attributed to the intrinsic spatial geometry of the Universe.} The SGLS+QSO data alone leave curvature essentially prior dominated, $Ω_K=0.70^{+6.33}_{-7.12}$, whereas removing only the off-diagonal cluster covariance moves the posterior to $Ω_K=5.21^{+2.46}_{-2.07}$. Thus the cluster anchor supplies most of the curvature constraining power and its covariance materially changes the posterior location.

### [2] [Environmental dependence in the halo model informed by the FLAMINGO simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23997)
*G. Martín Girardi,D. Rana,H. Hoekstra,J. Schaye,M. Schaller*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Recommendation: ★★★☆☆ (58/100) - 论文主题为星系-星系透镜（GGL）与晕模型的环境依赖，属于强引力透镜范畴，与用户核心领域有直接交集；但研究基于FLAMINGO流体模拟的统计晕模型建模，聚焦宇宙学参数推断与HOD偏差，而非用户重点关注的星系尺度强透镜、高红移星系动力学/多相气体观测，方法（模拟+统计建模）与用户偏好的观测/解析强透镜研究差异较大，作者群亦不在核心作者列表内。

TL;DR: 该论文利用FLAMINGO宇宙学流体模拟，构建欠密度与过密度区域的晕样本，检验环境对晕模型各要素的影响，并提出密度分拆（density-split）星系-星系透镜分析所需的模型修正方案。

Motivation: 密度分拆的星系-星系透镜（GGL）研究常用晕模型估计物理参数，但通常忽略环境效应；作者希望使晕模型能够适用于按环境分箱的GGL分析，并理解模型中各要素的环境依赖性。

Method: 基于FLAMINGO流体模拟套件，以1–10 h⁻¹Mpc的球形孔径内晕计数定义环境密度，区分欠密度与过密度区域的晕样本；考察晕质量函数（HMF）、晕偏置、恒星-晕质量关系（SHMR）、条件恒星质量函数与质量-浓度关系的环境依赖，并提出可施加于现有HMF公式的经验修正；同时评估忽略环境对HOD参数推断的影响。

Result: HMF与晕偏置对环境有强依赖，提出的经验修正可改善现有HMF公式的适用性；密度分拆后晕偏置随质量近似为常数；SHMR、条件恒星质量函数与质量-浓度关系亦存在环境依赖，但可用现有模型处理；忽略环境会导致SHMR与卫星分数的推断出现偏差。

Conclusion: 对按环境（以及与环境相关的形态、颜色等星系属性）分箱的样本开展晕模型分析时，需重新审视模型的基本假设，并纳入环境依赖的修正。

Abstract: Galaxy-galaxy lensing (GGL) measurements around galaxy samples in different environments provide valuable information about the galaxy-halo connection. In such studies the halo model is commonly used to estimate physical parameters from the lensing signal, but the impact of environment is generally not considered. Our aim is to adapt the halo model to allow a density-split GGL analysis and to understand the environmental dependence of the elements of the model. We use the FLAMINGO suite of cosmological hydrodynamical simulations to construct samples of halos in underdense and overdense regions. Environment density is assigned to halos based on halo counts within an aperture, employing different aperture sizes to probe both local and large scales. We utilize spherical apertures with radii between $1 h^{-1} {\rm Mpc}$ and $10 h^{-1} {\rm Mpc}$. We find a strong dependence on environment for the halo mass function (HMF) and the halo bias. To account for this, we propose an empirical modification that can be applied to existing HMF prescriptions. We find that after a density split, the halo bias is approximately constant with mass. There is also an environmental dependence for the stellar-to-halo-mass relation (SHMR), the conditional stellar mass function, and the mass-concentration relation, all of which can be addressed with existing models. We evaluate the impact of overlooking environment on halo occupation distribution (HOD) parameter inference, finding that it leads to biased estimations of the SHMR and the satellite fraction. These results emphasize the need to review the assumptions adopted in the halo model when working with samples of galaxies binned by environment, and consequently, by galaxy properties that correlate with environment, such as morphology and color.

Conclusion Translation:

我们利用 JWST/NIRSpec 积分场光谱（R$\sim$2700；0.97--5.14 $\mu$m）研究了四个邻近相互作用 (U)LIRGs 的空间分辨消光和冰柱密度。这一宽波段范围涵盖了大量（>25）H 复合线和 H$_2$ 振转发射线，以及显著的 H$_2$O（$\sim$3.02 $\mu$m）和 CO$_2$（$\sim$4.26 $\mu$m）冰吸收特征。我们发展了三种方法，利用 H 和 H$_2$ 发射线及冰吸收特征，推导电离气体（A$_{\text K}^{\text HII}$）和热分子气体（A$_{\text K}^{\text H_2}$）的相特定消光，以及 H$_2$O 和 CO$_2$ 冰柱密度（N$_{\text H_2O}$ 和 N$_{\text CO_2}$）。核区（0.2\" 半径）表现出比延展区更高的消光和冰柱密度，核区 A$_{\text K}^{\text HII}$ 和 A$_{\text K}^{\text H_2}$ 的中值分别为 1.25 和 0.75，而延展区为 0.61 和 0.50。电离气体比热分子气体消光更强，A$_{\text K}^{\text HII}\approx1.5$ A$_{\text K}^{\text H_2}$，而 H$_2$O 冰柱密度在两相之间相当。在一些核区，连续谱推导的 N$_{\text H_2O}$ 达到 $\sim10^{18.81}$ cm$^{-2}$，可能源于致密遮蔽成分。这些结果表明，电离气体起源于更深的嵌入区域，而水冰包裹两相气体，最难遮蔽的核区周围还有额外富冰物质。N$_{\text CO_2}$/N$_{\text H_2O}$ 丰度比的中值约为 $\sim0.10$，比典型银河系值低 2--3 倍。

Conclusion Translation:
基于可用摘要：电离气体起源于更深的嵌入区域，水冰包裹两相气体，最难遮蔽的核区外还有额外富冰物质；CO2/H2O比值偏低反映该环境下冰化学的差异。
