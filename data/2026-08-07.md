Abstract: Hub-filament systems are networks of converging interstellar filaments, often with active star formation at their centres, that may play an important role in high-mass star formation. In Anderson et al. (2021) we found that the mass fraction that ends up in a clump's most massive core is significantly higher in IR-dark hubs than IR-bright clumps, suggesting that the most-massive cores form early on. Such early massive core formation requires large inflow rates and dynamically active IR-dark clumps. We now present N$_2$H$^+$(J=1-0) observations of six IR-dark hub-filament systems mapped with ALMA 12m+7m+TP at $\sim 3''$ resolution, to trace the kinematics of the dense gas. The data show intricate emission structures and complex spectra. To characterise their kinematics, we have developed mwydyn, a fully-automated, multiple velocity component, hyperfine line-fitting code. Our results reveal that the emission invariably consists of quiescent individual filaments in the outskirts that converge towards the hub centres where a systematic increase in velocity dispersion and number of components is observed. We also find that the distribution of centroid velocities is remarkably similar between clumps, despite spanning more than one order of magnitude in mass. We propose that our results are best explained by the mixing of gravitationally-driven multi-directional inflows, resulting in highly complex and dynamic hub centres. We also discuss the implications of the observed differentiated filament and hub gas kinematics in the context of the 3D morphology of hub-filament systems.

### [2] [Clump Migration in Disk Galaxies: Revisiting Chandrasekhar's Dynamical Friction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06350)
*Pushpak Pandey,Kanak Saha*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

Recommendation: ★★☆☆☆ (35/100) - 该论文研究盘星系中恒星团块的动力学摩擦迁移，属于星系结构演化领域，与用户主要关注的强引力透镜及高红移星系动力学、分子气体等主题仅有间接关联。论文未涉及引力透镜方法或数据，也未包含用户核心作者。虽然涉及暗物质分布，但并非透镜研究，相关性较低。

TL;DR: 本文通过孤立盘星系模拟，追踪了九个长寿命恒星团块的轨道演化，发现八个向内迁移并损失60-90%的质量。经典Chandrasekhar动力学摩擦模型虽能捕捉迁移对质量和半径的整体依赖，但对六个团块的迁移时间高估2-10倍，表明团块间相互作用、非圆轨道和时变盘势等额外物理过程对团块迁移至关重要。

Motivation: 大质量恒星团块被认为通过动力学摩擦向星系中心迁移，贡献星系核球增长和盘结构演化。然而经典Chandrasekhar公式对嵌入真实星系盘中、延展且演化的团块是否仍然有效尚不确定，需要直接检验。

Method: 使用孤立盘星系模拟，开发了基于位置和质量的追踪算法来识别和跟踪长寿命恒星团块；将观测到的轨道演化与基于模拟重子和暗物质质量分布构建的动力学摩擦模型预测进行比较。

Result: 识别出九个长寿命团块（排除初始瞬态阶段后），其中八个向内迁移同时损失60-90%的初始质量。解析模型能重现迁移对团块质量和银心距的整体依赖，但对六个团块的迁移时间高估约2-10倍。由于质量损失会减弱动力学摩擦力，无法解释观测到的更快迁移。

Conclusion: 经典Chandrasekhar动力学摩擦公式不足以准确描述真实星系盘中延展且演化的恒星团块的迁移过程，团块间相互作用、非圆轨道以及时变盘势等额外物理机制在调控团块迁移中发挥重要作用。

Abstract: Massive stellar clumps are thought to migrate toward galactic centers through dynamical friction, contributing to bulge growth and the structural evolution of disk galaxies. While the classical Chandrasekhar formalism is widely used to estimate clump inspiral times, its validity for extended, evolving clumps embedded in realistic galactic disks remains uncertain. We test this formalism using an isolated disk galaxy simulation by identifying and tracking individual stellar clumps over multiple orbital periods. We develop a position- and mass-based tracking algorithm that follows long-lived clumps and compare their measured orbital evolution with predictions from a dynamical friction model constructed from the simulated baryonic and dark matter mass distributions. After excluding the initial transient phase, we identify nine long-lived clumps, eight of which migrate inward while losing 60-90\% of their initial mass. The analytical model reproduces the overall dependence of migration on clump mass and galactocentric radius, but for six clumps it overestimates the inspiral time by factors of $\sim2$-$10$. Since mass loss would reduce the dynamical friction force, it cannot account for the observed faster migration. Our results suggest that additional physical processes, for example clump-clump interactions, non-circular orbits, and the time-dependent disk potential play an important role in regulating clump migration beyond the assumptions of the classical Chandrasekhar formulation.

Abstract Translation:
大质量恒星团块被认为通过动力学摩擦向星系中心迁移，从而促进核球增长和盘状星系的结构演化。虽然经典的Chandrasekhar公式被广泛用于估计团块内迁时间，但其对于嵌入在真实星系盘中的延展且演化的团块的有效性仍不确定。我们利用孤立盘星系模拟来检验该公式，通过识别并追踪多个轨道周期内的单个恒星团块。我们开发了一种基于位置和质量的追踪算法，用于跟踪长寿命团块，并将其测量的轨道演化与基于模拟的重子和暗物质质量分布构建的动力学摩擦模型的预测进行比较。在排除初始瞬态阶段后，我们识别出九个长寿命团块，其中八个向内迁移，同时损失了其初始质量的60-90%。解析模型再现了迁移对团块质量和星系中心距离的整体依赖性，但对于六个团块，它高估了内迁时间约2-10倍。由于质量损失会减小动力学摩擦力，因此它不能解释观测到的更快迁移。我们的结果表明，额外的物理过程，例如团块-团块相互作用、非圆轨道以及时变盘势，在调节团块迁移中起着重要作用，超出了经典Chandrasekhar公式的假设。

Conclusion Translation:
基于现有摘要的结论：经典Chandrasekhar动力学摩擦公式不足以准确描述真实星系盘中延展且演化的恒星团块的迁移过程，团块间相互作用、非圆轨道以及时变盘势等额外物理机制在调控团块迁移中发挥重要作用。
