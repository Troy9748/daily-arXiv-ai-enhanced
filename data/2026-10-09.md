Abstract: The complex relationship between galaxies and diffuse gas at large scales depends strongly on astrophysical feedback and cosmology. The dispersion measure (DM) of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs) is a column of cosmic baryon density and is dominated by the intergalactic medium, which is not necessarily captured by traditional halo model prescriptions. We introduce a generative model to reconstruct 3D gas density fields from galaxy surveys, including redshift space distortions. The probabilistic model is trained on the CAMELS50 simulation suite (IllustrisTNG) and learns to predict the posterior distribution of gas fields conditioned on stellar mass-weighted galaxy density, cosmological parameters, the minimum stellar mass threshold for the galaxy sample, and the baryon suppression power spectrum as a measure of baryonic feedback. Our method uses the 3D gas reconstruction to estimate per-sightline FRB DMs and their posteriors. We validate the model on test boxes by showing agreement between predicted and true DM across individual lines of sight and the overall DM distribution. The model generalizes to a box size $\sim$\,4$^3$ times larger in volume than the training boxes and can cover the volumes spanned by cosmological FRB sightlines. The model's 3D gas density predictions vary with input power spectrum and feedback scenarios, offering an inference path to constrain feedback models from FRBs and other probes such as X-rays, the SZ effect, and weak lensing.

*Karen Martínez-Acosta,Nicolas Tejos,Ismael Pessa,J Xavier Prochaska,Joseph Burchett,Britton Smith,Jessica Werk,Nelson Padilla,Sebastián López*

Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆ (12/100) - 该论文研究低红移（z<1）星系团间宇宙网纤维中的WHIM重子成分，使用HST/COS类星体吸收光谱，属于宇宙学/星系际介质领域。与用户核心兴趣（强引力透镜、高红移星系动力学/运动学、多相气体、分子/致密气体、宇宙正午星系）几乎无交集，仅'气体'一词有表面重叠，不构成科学问题或方法上的实质关联。

Abstract: At redshift z<1, the $Λ$CDM paradigm predicts that $\sim$40-50\% of baryons reside in the warm-hot intergalactic medium (WHIM) within cosmic web filaments. Characterizing the physical state and distribution of this dominant baryonic component is crucial for a complete picture of baryon evolution in the low-redshift Universe. However, directly observing the WHIM has been challenging due to its low density and high temperature and ionization state. Absorption-line spectroscopy offers a promising approach, particularly through analyses of broad Ly-$α$ absorbers (BLAs; Doppler parameter b>40 km/s) and OVI absorbers. This work focuses on probing WHIM signatures by analyzing the incidence rates of these tracers in and around putative cosmic web filaments connecting massive galaxy clusters. We use far-ultraviolet (FUV) absorption spectroscopy from HST/COS along ten QSO sightlines at z<1, selected for their proximity in projected distance and velocity to cluster pairs. We focus on absorption features of WHIM tracers (BLAs and OVI) and cold-gas tracers (narrow Ly-$α$ absorbers, or NLAs; b<40 km/s), which are used as a control sample for colder gas. We measure excess incidence rates for total HI, NLAs, BLAs, and OVI within a rest-frame $Δv=\pm1000$ km/s and an impact parameter $Δd<3$ Mpc relative to the field expectation, and find that the excesses of BLAs and OVI are about twice those of NLAs. Additionally, the covering fractions of BLAs and OVI appear larger by factors of $\sim$1.7 and 1.8, respectively, than in a random field control sample, whereas the NLA covering fraction is consistent with the random expectation. The larger relative excess of WHIM tracers compared to cold-gas tracers near independent cluster pairs indicates the prevalence of warm-hot gas in these environments, representing a potential signature of WHIM detection in inter-cluster filaments.

Abstract: We present a multiwavelength analysis of NGC 4936, the central galaxy of a weak cool-core group hosting an unusually large cold/warm gas reservoir. NGC 4936 contains $\sim2\times10^8$ M$_\odot$ of molecular gas in a rotating disk of radius $\sim1$ kpc, but no detected H I, with a $3σ$ upper limit of $1.13\times10^7$ M$_\odot$ within $\sim2.5$ kpc. The resulting $H_2$/H I mass ratio $>17$ is consistent with a predominantly molecular medium in the high-pressure environments of central galaxies. SOAR imaging and MUSE spectroscopy reveal ionised gas extending to $\sim15$ kpc in a complex filamentary morphology that broadly follows the molecular gas kinematics while extending well beyond the CO disk. Dust filaments similarly extend to $\sim14$ kpc and appear to interact with a companion galaxy located only $\sim2.5$ kpc away in projection. NGC 4936 has a star formation rate of $0.29$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$, and its AGN shows sub-mm and X-ray variability. We favour atmospheric condensation as the primary origin of the molecular gas, although externally acquired gas may contribute. Our recalculated thermodynamic profiles reach $t_{\rm cool}/t_{\rm ff}\sim25$ in the innermost region, placing the system near the threshold for multiphase condensation. The ongoing interaction may perturb the atmosphere and promote condensation while shaping the morphology and kinematics of the multiphase medium, rather than directly supplying most of the central cold gas. We additionally detect 20 H I structures in the surrounding group environment, with masses of $\sim10^7$--$10^8$ M$_\odot$, including several with no obvious optical counterparts.

图1：完备度权重：上图显示了Subaru/HSC恒星数据的天球投影散布分布。红色虚线圆圈标出了恒星被纳入我们拟合和基于傅里叶分析的区域。恒星根据第2节所述人工星测试导出的完备度权重进行颜色编码。较深的紫色调表示完备度较高（权重较小）的恒星，而较浅的绿色调对应于需要较大完备度校正的恒星，主要位于更密集、更拥挤的区域。下图使用对数间隔的分箱显示了这些完备度权重的分布。

<small>Figure 1: Completeness weights: The top panel shows the sky-projected scatter distribution of the Subaru/HSC stellar data. The red dashed circle marks the region within which stars were included in our fits and Fourier-based analysis. Stars are colour-coded according to the completeness weights derived from the artificial-star tests described in Section 2 . Darker purple tones indicate stars with higher completeness (smaller weights), whereas lighter green tones correspond to stars requiring larger completeness corrections, predominantly in denser, more crowded regions. The bottom panel shows the distribution of these completeness weights using logarithmically spaced bins.</small>

#### Figure 2
![Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10941v1/full-plot-chi.png)

图2：应用于Draco：我们将Vitral et al. (2026b)的方法应用于Draco矮星系的Subaru/HSC数据，其中包含 \(\mathcal{O}(10^{4})\) 颗成员星。第一列显示观测到的投影笛卡尔密度图，其中颜色和透明度均随局部相对恒星表面密度变化；红色虚线椭圆标出拟合的投影半数目椭圆。第二列分为两行。上一行显示径向表面密度轮廓（绿色）作为距星系中心投影距离的函数，以Vitral et al. (2026b) 式A1中定义的标度半径为单位的表示，以及最佳拟合轴对称Plummer模型（红色）。下一行显示相应的径向残差，定义为观测与拟合的 \(\Sigma(R)\) 轮廓之比，并揭示出微弱的波纹图案。此列中的两个分箱轮廓仅作为拟合优度诊断显示，不进入拟合过程，拟合直接基于单颗恒星位置进行。第三列和第四列分别展示 \(\chi\) 场的方位角平均一维功率谱和相应的低通滤波 \(\chi\) 图。残差功率谱显示出低频功率过剩，在滤波后的 \(\chi\) 图中反映为类似Peñarrubia et al. (2025)和Vitral et al. (2026b)受控子晕实验中恢复出的大尺度环状涨落，其中蓝色表示相对于平滑Plummer基线的亏缺，红色表示恒星过剩。这使得Draco成为更深度后续观测的特别有吸引力的案例。

<small>Figure 2: Application to Draco: We apply the methodology of Vitral et al. (2026b) to Subaru/HSC data of the Draco dwarf galaxy, which contain \(\mathcal{O}(10^{4})\) member stars. The first column shows the observed projected Cartesian density map, where both colour and transparency scale with the local relative stellar surface density; the red dashed ellipse marks the fitted projected half-number ellipse. The second column is divided into two rows. The top row shows the radial surface density profile (green) as a function of projected distance from the galaxy centre, expressed in units of the scale radius defined in Eq. A1 of Vitral et al. (2026b) , together with the best-fitting axisymmetric Plummer model (red). The bottom row shows the corresponding radial residuals, defined as the ratio between the observed and fitted \(\Sigma(R)\) profiles, and reveals a faint corrugated pattern. Both binned profiles in this column are shown solely as goodness-of-fit diagnostics and do not enter the fitting procedure, which is performed directly on the individual stellar positions. The third and fourth columns present the azimuthally averaged one-dimensional power spectrum of the \(\chi\) field and the corresponding low-pass filtered \(\chi\) map, respectively. The residual power spectrum shows an excess of low-frequency power, reflected in the filtered \(\chi\) map as large-scale, ring-like fluctuations similar to those recovered in the controlled subhalo experiments of Peñarrubia et al. (2025) and Vitral et al. (2026b) , where blue indicates depletion with respect to the smooth Plummer baseline, and red indicates stellar excess. This makes Draco a particularly compelling case for deeper follow-up.</small>
