### [1] [The two faces of tides: gravitational instability during galaxy mergers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04528)
*Trisha Khan,Ayush Hazarika*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

Recommendation: ★★☆☆☆ (45/100) - 该论文研究星系并合中的潮汐场对金斯不稳定性的影响，涉及弥散气体和致密分子气体，与用户兴趣中的分子气体和致密气体有一定关联，但并非强引力透镜研究，也未涉及高红移星系动力学或透镜方法。

TL;DR: 本文发展了一个解析框架，研究伴星系潮汐场如何修改经典金斯不稳定性，发现潮汐场在临界角约54.7°处分隔了破坏性和压缩性两种机制，并显著影响弥散气体的引力坍缩，但致密分子云中恢复经典金斯极限。

Motivation: 引力不稳定性是星际气体坍缩的基本机制，但在星系合并等动态演化环境中，外部潮汐场可能显著改变坍缩条件，目前对潮汐场各向异性和时间依赖性如何修改经典金斯不稳定性的理解不足。

Method: 开发了一个解析框架，将伴星系潮汐场的各向异性和时间依赖性纳入色散关系，分析径向潮汐分量对金斯不稳定性的修改，并考虑气体密度和星系间距等因素的影响。

Result: 发现临界角θ_c≈54.7°可区分破坏性和压缩性潮汐机制；破坏性潮汐增大特征尺度并限制不稳定模式范围，压缩性潮汐扩展不稳定谱并增加扰动增长率；潮汐效应在弥散介质中显著，在致密分子云中可忽略；所有潮汐效应在近心点附近最强，弥散气体的潮汐辅助坍缩滞后近心点约一个自由落体时间。

Conclusion: 伴星系诱导的潮汐场在调节星系合并中弥散星际气体的引力不稳定性尺度和效率方面起关键作用。

Abstract: Gravitational instability is a fundamental mechanism driving collapse of interstellar gas, yet in dynamically evolving environments such as galaxy mergers, external tidal fields can significantly alter the conditions for collapse. In this work, we develop an analytical framework to investigate how the tidal field of a companion galaxy modifies the classical Jeans instability by incorporating its anisotropic and time-dependent nature into the dispersion relation. We find that the tidal field introduces a critical angle between the cloud's position vector and the merger axis, $θ_c \approx 54.7^\circ$, separating disruptive and compressive regimes of the radial tidal component. Disruptive tides suppress instability by increasing the characteristic length scale and restricting the range of unstable modes, whereas compressive tides enhance collapse by extending the unstable spectrum and increasing the growth rate of perturbations. The impact of tidal fields depends sensitively on gas density, being significant in diffuse media but negligible in dense molecular environments where the classical Jeans limit is recovered. All tidal effects peak near pericentric passage due to the strong dependence on galaxy separation. Since the free-fall time of diffuse gas is comparable to the duration of the close passage, tidally assisted collapse in the diffuse medium lags pericentre by roughly a free-fall time, whereas dense gas responds essentially instantaneously. These results demonstrate that companion-induced tidal fields play a key role in regulating the scale and efficiency of gravitational instability of diffuse interstellar gas in galaxy mergers. The code underlying this work is publicly available.

引力不稳定性是驱动星际气体坍缩的基本机制，然而在星系合并等动态演化环境中，外部潮汐场可以显著改变坍缩的条件。在本工作中，我们发展了一个解析框架，通过将伴星系的潮汐场各向异性和时间依赖性纳入色散关系，研究其如何修改经典的Jeans不稳定性。我们发现，潮汐场引入了一个临界角，即云的位置矢量与合并轴之间的夹角 $θ_c \approx 54.7^\circ$，该角度将径向潮汐分量区分为破坏性和压缩性两种状态。破坏性潮汐通过增加特征长度尺度并限制不稳定模式的范围来抑制不稳定性，而压缩性潮汐则通过扩展不稳定谱并增加扰动的增长率来增强坍缩。潮汐场的影响对气体密度敏感，在弥散介质中显著，但在致密分子环境中可忽略，此时恢复经典的Jeans极限。所有潮汐效应在近心点附近达到峰值，这归因于其对星系间距的强依赖性。由于弥散气体的自由落体时间与近距离通过持续时间相当，弥散介质中潮汐辅助的坍缩滞后于近心点约一个自由落体时间，而致密气体则几乎瞬时响应。这些结果表明，伴星系诱导的潮汐场在调节星系合并中弥散星际气体的引力不稳定性尺度和效率方面起着关键作用。本工作所依据的代码公开可用。

基于提供的摘要，本工作的结论可总结为：伴星系诱导的潮汐场在调节星系合并中弥散星际气体的引力不稳定性尺度和效率方面起关键作用。具体而言，潮汐场通过引入临界角 $θ_c \approx 54.7^\circ$ 区分破坏性和压缩性效应，破坏性潮汐抑制不稳定性，而压缩性潮汐增强坍缩；其影响依赖于气体密度，在弥散介质中显著，在致密分子环境中可忽略；所有潮汐效应在近心点附近达到峰值，且弥散气体的响应滞后约一个自由落体时间，而致密气体则几乎瞬时响应。

Abstract: For decades, the Orion Bar has been the prototypical photo-dissociation region. Viewed nearly edge-on, it offers a unique window into the stratified chemical structure of the atomic-to-molecular transition of the interstellar medium. Understanding its photochemistry is essential to interpreting key observations originating from dense photo-dissociation regions. ALMA and JWST observations reveal that H2 photo-dissociation front overlaps with the C+ recombination front and exhibits a complex spatial morphology. Despite considerable theoretical effort, existing modeling approaches based on simplified geometrical assumptions have difficulties reproducing the spatial emission structure. Our aim is to investigate the response of photochemistry in realistic three-dimensional density distributions, using the Orion Bar as a representative application. We present the first fully three-dimensional high-resolution model of an Orion Bar analogue that resolves the relevant photochemical fronts using the upgraded steady-state 3D-PDR photo-dissociation region code, which allows for the treatment of plane-irradiatation and for the solution of the non-LTE H2 rovibrational levels. We find that the H2 dissociation front is characterized by a complex surface that overlaps with the C+ recombination front. Our 3D model can reproduce the complex morphology of H2 emission seen in observations, in particular the arc- and filament-like features, and provides a physical explanation of its chemistry. The overlapping H2 dissociation and C+ recombination fronts and the spatial emission morphology can be explained due to the three-dimensional gas distribution, resulting in shadowing and shielding by dense substructures. Our results mark a turning point for astrochemistry, where three-dimensional steady-state models can deliver fundamentally new insights into the chemistry of the interstellar medium.

Motivation: 再电离时代是宇宙演化中的关键时期，但目前对其持续时间和中点的观测约束仍然不足。作者希望通过开发新的观测探针来更好地理解再电离过程。

Method: 利用AMBER模拟，将kSZ场平方与CMB透镜势进行互相关分析，用以探测再电离信号；并对Simons Observatory类似实验和CMB-HD等未来实验进行了观测预测，同时探讨了前景污染、透镜污染及估计器偏差等挑战及缓解方案。

Result: 对于Simons Observatory类实验（5μK-arcmin白噪声，40%天区覆盖），信号可以以S/N=2至3的边缘水平被探测到；而CMB-HD类未来实验可达到S/N~50，能够对再电离情景提供有信息量的约束。

Conclusion: kSZ平方场与CMB透镜的互相关是一种有前景的再电离探测手段，未来的高灵敏度CMB实验将能够对该信号进行高信噪比测量，从而更好地约束再电离的历史。

Abstract: We investigate the cross-correlation of the kinematic Sunyaev-Zeldovich (kSZ) effect with lensing of the cosmic microwave background (CMB) as a probe of the epoch of reionisation. During reionisation, bubbles of ionised electrons form around overdensities, generating temperature perturbations in the CMB via the kSZ effect, which correlate with the projected matter density field probed by CMB lensing. We demonstrate using AMBER simulations that this effect can be probed via cross-correlating the squared kSZ field with the CMB lensing potential, and that the signal is sensitive to the duration and midpoint of reionisation. We forecast that for a Simons Observatory-like experiment with 5$μ$K-arcmin white noise, covering 40% of the sky, the signal could be marginally detected at $S/N$ = 2 to 3. Meanwhile, a futuristic experiment like CMB-HD could provide a measurement of $S/N \sim 50$, yielding informative constraints on reionisation scenarios. We investigate potential challenges in measuring the signal and explore mitigation for each: contamination from extragalactic foregrounds at low redshift, contamination of the kSZ-squared estimator by lensing, and other estimator biases.

Abstract Translation:
我们研究了运动学Sunyaev-Zeldovich（kSZ）效应与宇宙微波背景（CMB）透镜的互相关，作为再电离时代的探针。在再电离期间，电离电子气泡在过密度周围形成，通过kSZ效应在CMB中产生温度扰动，这些扰动与CMB透镜所探测的投影物质密度场相关。我们使用AMBER模拟证明，可以通过将kSZ平方场与CMB透镜势进行互相关来探测该效应，并且该信号对再电离的持续时间和中点敏感。我们预测，对于类似Simons Observatory的实验，具有5μK-arcmin白噪声，覆盖40%的天空，该信号可以以信噪比S/N = 2到3被边缘探测。同时，像CMB-HD这样的未来实验可以提供S/N ~ 50的测量，从而对再电离情景产生有信息量的约束。我们研究了测量该信号时可能面临的挑战，并探讨了每种挑战的缓解措施：低红移河外前景的污染、透镜对kSZ平方估计器的污染，以及其他估计器偏差。

基于现有摘要，结论为：kSZ平方场与CMB透镜的互相关是一种有前景的再电离探测手段，未来的高灵敏度CMB实验将能够对该信号进行高信噪比测量，从而更好地约束再电离的历史。

#### Figure 6
![Figure 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04868v1/plots/websky_fg_bias_aso.jpg)

图6：由于河外前景tSZ、CIB和射电点源（即不包括低红移kSZ）导致的\(C_{L}^{K\phi}\)的分数偏差，从websky模拟估计。y轴在\([-10,10]\)范围内是线性的，否则是对数。作为指导，灰色阴影区域表示\([-1,1]\)。我们假设类似Simons Observatory的实验设置，详见第4.3节。对于实线，我们假设可以移除145 GHz处通量\(>3\unit{mJY}\)的点源；虚线假设更乐观的阈值\(0.5\unit{mJY}\)。对于四个频率的简单谐波ILC，前景偏差很大，在低和高\(L\)处大于两个数量级。虽然单独对tSZ或CIB进行（频率）光谱模板去投影没有帮助，但从进入二次估计器\(\hat{K}\)的一个或两个“腿”（即温度图）同时去投影两者，可以显著减少污染。由于这些前景几乎完全来自低于再电离时代的红移，这些偏差将通过使用例如CIB或星系巡天“清洁”低红移透镜而显著减少 - 见第4.2节的讨论。

<small>Figure 6: Fractional biases to \(C_{L}^{K\phi}\) due to the extragalactic foregrounds tSZ, CIB and radio point sources (i.e. not including low-redshift kSZ), estimated from the websky simulations. The y-axis is scale is linear in the range \([-10,10]\) and logarithmic otherwise. As a guide the grey shaded region indicates \([-1,1]\) . We assume an Simons Observatory-like experimental setup, see Section 4.3 for details. For solid lines we assume point sources with flux \(>3\unit{mJY}\) at 145 GHz can be removed; dashed lines assume a more optimistic threshold of \(0.5\unit{mJY}\) . For a simple harmonic ILC of the four frequencies, the foreground bias is large, greater than two orders of magnitude at low and high \(L\) . While de-projecting (frequency) spectral templates for either the tSZ or CIB alone is not helpful, deprojecting both, from one or both “legs" (i.e. temperature maps) entering the quadratic estimator \(\hat{K}\) , reduces the contamination significantly. Since these foregrounds are almost completely sourced from redshifts lower than the epoch of reionisation, these biases would be significantly reduced by “cleaning" low-redshift lensing using e.g. the CIB or galaxy surveys - see discussion in Section 4.2 .</small>

图7：由于透镜对\(\hat{K}\)估计器的污染导致的\(C_{L}^{K\phi}\)的分数偏差。带开放符号的虚线表示负偏差。圆圈/蓝色线使用正常的\(\hat{K}\)估计器，并产生约1的分数偏差。对于绿色线/向下三角形，\(K\)是从未透镜的CMB温度估计的，因此这种透镜偏差消失。对于橙色线/向上三角形，使用“透镜硬化”的\(\hat{K}\)估计器，将透镜偏差减少到10%。当估计的\(K\)与重建的\(\phi\)（而不是真实的\(\phi\)）互相关时，偏差只有小幅增加（红色线/正方形）。

<small>Figure 7: The fractional bias to \(C_{L}^{K\phi}\) due to lensing contamination of the \(\hat{K}\) estimator. Dashed lines with open symbols indicate a negative bias. For the green line/downward triangles, \(K\) is estimated from a CMB temperature that is unlensed, hence this lensing bias vanishes.
