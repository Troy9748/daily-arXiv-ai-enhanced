However, at redshifts $z>1$, only few dual AGN are confirmed at projected separations $\gtrsim$ 1 kpc for the two SMBHs. Result: 张力由基准宇宙学驱动，范围从2.08σ到远超5σ，而超新星汇编的影响最多改变1.2σ。判别力强烈不对称：Pantheon+SH0ES宇宙与所有测试背景都不一致，显著性远超5σ，即便对近期族群模型预期的10–20个事件仍是3–5σ；而H0仅相差0.24 km/s/Mpc的Planck 2018与Union3基准，任何目录规模都无法区分。

Conclusion: LISA明亮标准汽笛是Cepheid校准情景的尖锐仲裁者，但在当前误差预算下对Planck 2018与Union3两种情景保持沉默。

Abstract: Gravitational waves from massive black hole binaries detected by LISA will deliver luminosity distances without any astrophysical calibration, offering a probe of the expansion history independent of the cosmic distance ladder. Rather than asking how tightly LISA will constrain a single fiducial cosmology, we ask which of several mutually discrepant present-day cosmologies a future LISA dataset would render most self-consistent. We build mock catalogues of LISA bright sirens under three fiducial cosmologies drawn from datasets that participate in the current $H_0$ discrepancy (Planck 2018, Union3 and Pantheon+SH0ES), with a self-consistent error budget from instrumental noise, weak lensing and host peculiar velocities. We compare the posterior from the simulated LISA data alone with that of a real-data combination of Type Ia supernovae, DESI baryon acoustic oscillations, cosmic chronometers and the compressed Planck 2018 CMB, and quantify their internal tension in the $(H_0,Ω_m)$ plane, crossing each fiducial with three SN Ia compilations. The fiducial drives the tension, from $2.08σ$ to well beyond $5σ$, while the supernova compilation changes it by at most $1.2σ$. The discriminating power is strongly asymmetric: a Pantheon+SH0ES universe is inconsistent with every background we test, at a significance far in excess of $5σ$ and still at $3$-$5σ$ for the $10$-$20$ events expected from recent population models, whereas the Planck 2018 and Union3 fiducials, which differ by only $0.24$ km/s/Mpc in $H_0$, cannot be separated by any catalogue size. LISA bright sirens are therefore a sharp arbiter of the Cepheid-calibrated scenario and, on this error budget, silent between the other two.

表 4：LISA-only 后验与每个真实数据背景之间的内部张力，覆盖三个基准乘以三个 SN Ia 汇编的完整网格。\(Q_{DM}\) 是 Raveri–Hu 均值差统计量 Raveri and Hu (2019)，由两个后验均值之差及其协方差之和构建；\(n_{\sigma}\) (2D) 通过两个自由度的 \(\chi^{2}\) 分布将其转换。一维条目仅供参考，并且严重低估差异，因为两个后验都沿 \(H_{0}\) – \(\Omega_{m,0}\) 简并方向拉长，而它们之间的偏移横跨该方向；不应将它们读作结果。行按基准分组。沿一组向下读，询问在该基准模拟的目录会与哪个汇编一致；在固定汇编下跨组读，则分离出基准的效应。第二种比较才是有信息量的：基准是驱动张力的因素，汇编对其的改变至多为 \(1.2\sigma\)。当二维 \(n_{\sigma}\) 在形式上超过 \(\sim 5\) 时，我们仅报告为“ \(\gg 5\) ”，因为从 \(Q_{DM}\) 进行高斯尾部转换在那么远处没有意义，该值应被读作强烈不相容，而不是精确数字（见第 5.2 节）。

Where the two-dimensional \(n_{\sigma}\) would formally exceed \(\sim 5\) , we report it only as “ \(\gg 5\) ”, since the Gaussian-tail conversion from \(Q_{DM}\) is not meaningful that far out and the value should be read as strong incompatibility rather than as a precise number (see Section 5.2 ).</small>
